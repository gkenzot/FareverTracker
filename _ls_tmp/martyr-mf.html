Attack

Mania
0.85s
  • R1 Start Smashes the ground and deals 85.8% Strength Physical damage to enemies, knocking them back and increasing your Cooldown Reduction by 2% for 12s, stacking up to 3 times.
  • R2 20 kills Damage increased by 25% if you hit 2 enemies or more.
  • R3 40 kills While at max stacks, reduces the cooldown of all your weapon skills by 1.5s.

Skills

Wild Whirlwind
15s CD
  • R1 Start Spins around for 3s, dealing 21% Strength Physical damage every 0.25s. The last hit knocks enemies back. You can move freely during the channeling.
  • R2 20 kills Damage increased by 15%.
  • R3 40 kills If this critically strikes, it reduces the cooldown by 0.25s.
Outburst
30s CD
  • R1 Start Grants 10% Move Speed Factor and 6% Physical Mastery to allies within 40 for 6s.
  • R2 20 kills Grants 10% Move Speed Factor and 6% Physical Mastery.
  • R3 40 kills Dealing direct damage increases the duration of the effect by 6s.
Anger Release
2s
  • R1 Start Smashes the ground and releases a massive nova dealing 240% Strength Physical damage to all enemies and knocking them back.
  • R2 20 kills Smashes the ground and releases a massive nova dealing 240% Strength Physical damage to all enemies and knocking them back.
  • R3 40 kills Smashes the ground and releases a massive nova dealing 240% Strength Physical damage to all enemies and knocking them back.

Passive

Hysteria
  • R1 Start Each time you deal non-periodic Physical damage to an enemy, you gain a stack of Hysteria. At 150 stacks, you can release your anger.
  • R2 20 kills Now requires 100 stacks.
  • R3 40 kills Releasing anger slows enemies down for 50% for 5s.

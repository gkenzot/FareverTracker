Upgrade Stations can be found in [[Navelin]] and [[Tyrna]]. They allow you to increase your weapon's star rating, increasing its stats and unlocking a bonus passive at 3 stars.

When upgrading a weapon, it shows a side-by-side preview of the current and upgraded stats before committing. Stat increases are shown in green with the delta in brackets..

=== Upgrade Materials ===

* {{Kw|name=Spark Dust}}— obtained by defeating sparkling mobs, chests, or completing world activities
* {{Kw|name=Spark Shard}}— obtained by recycling weapons of Rare quality or higher.
* {{Kw|name=Spark Crystal}} — required for ★★★★ and ★★★★★ upgrades only. Currently only available as a reward from the [https://icecavebear.github.io/farever-map/#@2066.5,3329.0,0.0,RHVuZ2VvbnMsT3JiIGNoZXN0cw Lee Van Glyph] quest (1x{{Kw|name=Spark Crystal}} for 10x {{Kw|name=Ephemeral Heart}}). Can be completed once per character.
